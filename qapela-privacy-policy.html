1. Introduction

Qapela ("Qapela," "we," "us," or "our") connects Nigerian businesses with people willing to complete tasks, and separately runs an affiliate marketplace and an unreleased-music marketplace. This policy explains what information we collect across the platform, why we collect it, and what control you have over it.

By creating a Qapela account or using any part of the platform, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you don't agree, please don't use Qapela.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Account details — name, email or phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Profile information — display name, and for businesses, a company name.
  • Bank account details — account number, bank name, and the resolved account name, provided whenever you request a withdrawal, create an affiliate listing, or create an affiliate link. These are used to send your payout; see Section 4.
  • Task submissions — screenshots or written responses you submit as proof of completing a task. Screenshots are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Uploaded content — audio files for musicians listing unreleased tracks, and any listing details businesses provide for the affiliate marketplace.
  • Communications — anything you send us directly, including dispute descriptions and fraud reports.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Basic activity data tied to your account — campaigns created, tasks submitted, wallet transactions, referral activity — needed for the platform to function.
  • We do not currently run analytics or advertising trackers on Qapela.

2.3 Information from third parties

When you make or receive a payment, our payment partner shares transaction status and, where relevant, the verified name on a bank account back to us. We do not receive your card number or full bank login details — our payment partner handles that directly.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account, including switching between worker and business roles on the same account.
  • To operate the core platform: matching workers to campaigns, processing task submissions, calculating and paying rewards, referral commissions, affiliate commissions, and music sales.
  • To process payments and withdrawals.
  • To verify bank account ownership before a withdrawal or payout is sent.
  • To detect and investigate fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Use.
  • To respond to disputes you raise and to KYC verification you go through.
  • To send account-related notifications (task status, withdrawal status, dispute updates) inside the app.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, for any reason.

4. Payments & Financial Data

All money movement on Qapela — activation fees, wallet top-ups, campaign funding, withdrawals, affiliate payouts, and music sales — runs through our licensed payment partner. Qapela does not store your card details.

What we storeWhy
Bank account number & bank nameTo route your withdrawal or payout to the right account
Resolved account nameShown to you before you confirm a withdrawal, so you can catch a typo before money moves

Qapela does not currently store card numbers, CVVs, or full bank login credentials under any circumstance. That data is handled entirely by our payment partner.

5. How We Share Information

We share information only where the platform genuinely requires it:

  • With our payment partner — to process payments, verify bank accounts, and send withdrawals.
  • Between users, where the platform's function requires it — a business sees the display name of a worker who submitted to their campaign, along with the proof submitted. A worker sees a campaign's requirements and the business behind it. Affiliate and music-marketplace transactions are between the relevant parties only.
  • With law enforcement or regulators — only where we're legally required to, such as in response to a valid court order.
  • In a business transfer — if Qapela is ever acquired or merged, user information may transfer as part of that deal, subject to the same protections described here.

We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed, never stored in plain text.
  • All data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Cloudflare D1), with access restricted to what the platform's own code needs to function.
  • Task-proof screenshots are automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after upload.
  • Admin access to platform-wide data (user status, dispute resolution, financial oversight) is restricted to designated admin accounts only.

No system is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately.

7. Data Retention

  • Task-proof screenshots: deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Unreleased song audio files: removed automatically once a track's release date passes, or immediately if the musician manually unpublishes it.
  • Account and transaction records: retained for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward to comply with financial recordkeeping obligations and resolve any outstanding disputes.
  • You can request deletion of your account at any time — see Section 8.

8. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access — you can view most of your own data directly in the app: wallet history, submissions, referrals, and profile details.
  • Correction — you can update your name, email, phone number and password, and (for businesses) your company name, yourself in Account settings.
  • Deletion — you can delete your account and personal details yourself in Account settings, any time your wallet is empty and nothing is pending. Financial records tied to completed transactions may be retained where we have a legal obligation to keep them.
  • Withdrawal of consent — you can stop using Qapela at any time. Some data (like completed transaction history) can't be un-shared retroactively once a payment has been processed.

9. Children's Privacy

Qapela is open to everyone, including children and teenagers as well as adults. We only collect what is needed to run an account, and we never sell anyone's information. Anyone can see, correct, or delete their account data themselves in Account settings.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Qapela after a change means you accept the updated policy.
