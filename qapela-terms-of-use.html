7. Affiliate Marketplace

  • Businesses list products with a price and their own bank details for payout. Workers create promotional links, adding their own commission on top and providing their own bank details.
  • Customers checking out through an affiliate link see one combined price — the product price plus the worker's commission — and are never shown the breakdown.
  • On a successful sale, Qapela sends two separate bank transfers: one to the business (product price minus Qapela's platform fee) and one to the worker (their full commission, which Qapela never takes a cut of).
  • If a payout transfer fails on either side, the sale is flagged for manual review and resolution — the sale itself is still recorded and Qapela will work to resolve the failed transfer.
  • Businesses are responsible for the accuracy of their own listings and for actually fulfilling orders placed through them — Qapela facilitates the payment split, not the fulfillment of physical or digital goods.

  • Qapela takes a percentage-based platform fee from each sale; the musician keeps the rest, credited to their wallet.
